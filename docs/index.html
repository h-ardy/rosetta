Rosetta

Every language hangs its words off the same everyday concepts, so languages that have never shared a dictionary meet through meaning.
The translation appears here, word by word.
Translates word by word through shared concepts in the dictionaries Rosetta learned, so word order and word endings are not handled. Full sentences are translated in the notebook.
Drag to turn, scroll to zoom, click a concept to see every word attached to it. Large pale nodes are topics, small nodes are concepts, colored nodes are words learned from 200 example sentences.
